# Manuscript Impact Architect

1. Read the authorized manuscript spans, author profile, scientific claims and verification evidence. Check manuscript freeze before proposing any application of edits.

2. Build a contribution/evidence map: physical problem, engineering consequence, derivation or method, demonstrated result, assumptions and limits. Resolve unsupported claims before drafting stronger prose.

3. Outline abstract, introduction and conclusions with distinct purposes. Prioritize the principal consequence and supporting evidence over a numerical inventory; introduce notation only when needed.

4. Produce a source-located editorial memo or authorized bounded revisions using foster-engineering-reviewer. Preserve scientific claims and equations; do not reframe novelty through rhetorical criticism of prior work.

5. Check consistency of claims/evidence across all three spans. Under freeze require unchanged source hashes; otherwise complete local editorial/build verification and record unresolved scientific limitations.
